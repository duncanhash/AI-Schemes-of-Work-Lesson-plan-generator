PROJECT GUIDE: SUSTAINABLE WATER HARVESTING

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Title Learning Area Grade Duration Group Size Term
Sustainable Water Harvesting Science and Technology Grade 8 4 Weeks 4-5 learners per group Term 2

RATIONALE

Water scarcity remains a significant challenge in many parts of Kenya, impacting agriculture, sanitation, and daily life. This project empowers learners to understand the importance of water conservation and design practical solutions by engaging them in building rainwater harvesting models. By exploring sustainable methods, learners contribute to addressing local water challenges and fostering environmental stewardship within their communities.

SPECIFIC LEARNING OUTCOMES

  1. Investigate the impact of water scarcity on communities and the environment in Kenya.
  2. Identify various methods of water conservation and rainwater harvesting applicable to local contexts.
  3. Design and construct a functional rainwater harvesting model using available materials.
  4. Explain the scientific principles behind rainwater harvesting and water purification.
  5. Communicate effectively the importance and benefits of sustainable water management practices.
  6. Collaborate with peers to solve practical problems related to resource management.

SAFETY GUIDELINES

  • Always work under the direct supervision of the facilitator, especially when using cutting tools or handling electrical components (if any).
  • Wear appropriate protective gear such as gloves when handling soil, pipes, or other materials that may have sharp edges or dirt.
  • Handle all cutting tools (scissors, utility knives) with extreme care. Always cut away from your body and keep fingers clear of blades.
  • Ensure your workspace is tidy and free from clutter to prevent trips and falls.
  • Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water before and after handling materials, especially soil samples.
  • Do not taste or drink any water collected or filtered by the model without explicit instructions from the facilitator.
  • Report any broken tools, damaged materials, or accidents to the facilitator immediately.
  • Be mindful of your group members' safety and surroundings when moving around with materials.
