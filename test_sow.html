Week Lesson Strand Sub-strand Specific Learning Outcomes Key Inquiry Questions Core Competencies, Values & PCIs Learning Resources Assessment Method Remarks
1 1 Living Things Classification of Living Things Identify the characteristics of living organisms. What makes something living or non-living? Critical Thinking, Problem Solving, Communication; Respect, Responsibility; Environmental Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, charts of living organisms, pictures, locally available plants/animals. Oral questions, Observation. Introduction to the unit.
1 2 Living Things Classification of Living Things Describe the different levels of classification of living things. How do scientists group organisms? Digital Literacy, Communication; Curiosity, Orderliness; Citizenship. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, classification charts, internet access for research. Written exercise, Group discussion. Focus on Kingdom, Phylum, Class, Order, Family, Genus, Species.
2 3 Living Things Living Things in their Environment Explain the concept of an ecosystem and its components. What makes up an ecosystem? Critical Thinking, Cooperation; Empathy, Responsibility; Environmental Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, diagrams of ecosystems, local environment for observation. Practical (field observation), Oral questions. Distinguish biotic and abiotic components.
2 4 Living Things Living Things in their Environment Illustrate different types of food chains and food webs. How is energy transferred in an ecosystem? Creativity, Communication; Honesty, Accountability; Environmental Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, pictures of food chains/webs, drawing materials. Portfolio (drawings), Written exercise. Emphasize producers, consumers, decomposers.
3 5 Force and Energy Forms of Energy Identify various forms of energy in daily life. Where do we encounter energy in our daily lives? Critical Thinking, Self-Efficacy; Responsibility, Curiosity; Financial Literacy. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, pictures of appliances, objects demonstrating energy forms. Oral questions, Observation. Introduce heat, light, sound, electrical, mechanical energy.
3 6 Force and Energy Energy Transformation Demonstrate the transformation of energy from one form to another. How can energy change from one form to another? Problem Solving, Creativity; Resourcefulness, Collaboration; Life Skills Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, simple apparatus (e.g., torch, toy car, bell), locally available materials. Practical activity, Written report. Simple experiments to show energy conversion.
4 7 Force and Energy Sources of Energy Compare renewable and non-renewable sources of energy. Why are some energy sources better for the environment than others? Critical Thinking, Communication; Responsibility, Environmental awareness; Environmental Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, charts on energy sources, videos. Group presentation, Oral questions. Discuss advantages and disadvantages.
4 8 Force and Energy Sources of Energy Propose ways of conserving energy at home and in school. How can we use energy wisely? Creativity, Problem Solving; Responsibility, Patriotism; Environmental Education, Life Skills Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, case studies on energy conservation. Written exercise, Project (poster/brochure). Encourage practical application.
5 9 Matter States of Matter Describe the three states of matter based on particle arrangement and movement. What makes solids, liquids, and gases different? Critical Thinking, Digital Literacy; Curiosity, Accuracy; Financial Literacy. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, models of particles, water, ice, air in a syringe. Oral questions, Written exercise. Use diagrams to explain particle theory.
5 10 Matter Changes of State Explain the processes involved in changes of state (melting, freezing, evaporation, condensation, sublimation). How does matter change from one state to another? Problem Solving, Cooperation; Patience, Precision; Environmental Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, ice, hot plate, beakers, thermometer. Practical investigation, Written report. Conduct experiments to observe changes.
6 11 Matter Mixtures and Solutions Differentiate between pure substances, mixtures, and solutions. What makes a substance pure or a mixture? Critical Thinking, Communication; Orderliness, Curiosity; Life Skills Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, water, salt, sand, sugar, oil, beakers. Practical (mixing substances), Oral questions. Introduce homogeneous and heterogeneous mixtures.
6 12 Matter Separation of Mixtures Demonstrate various methods of separating mixtures. How can we separate different components from a mixture? Problem Solving, Creativity; Resourcefulness, Collaboration; Life Skills Education, Environmental Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, sieves, magnets, filter paper, funnels, separating funnel, sand-iron filings mixture, salt solution. Practical (separation techniques), Portfolio (record of procedures). Filtration, decantation, evaporation, magnetism.
7 13 MID-TERM REVIEW / ASSESSMENT Comprehensive review of Week 1-6 topics.
7 14 Living Things Human Body Systems Identify the main organs of the digestive system and their functions. How does our body process food? Communication, Self-Efficacy; Responsibility, Health Consciousness; Health Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, human torso model, charts of digestive system. Oral questions, Labelling exercise. Post mid-term topic.
8 15 Living Things Human Body Systems Describe common diseases affecting the digestive system and preventive measures. What can we do to keep our digestive system healthy? Critical Thinking, Problem Solving; Responsibility, Care for others; Health Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, health pamphlets, internet resources. Group discussion, Written exercise. Focus on hygiene and balanced diet.
8 16 Living Things Plant Structure and Function Identify the main parts of a flowering plant and their functions. What are the essential parts of a plant and what do they do? Observation, Critical Thinking; Curiosity, Appreciation for nature; Environmental Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, actual flowering plants, charts. Practical (plant dissection/observation), Oral questions. Roots, stem, leaves, flowers.
9 17 Force and Energy Forces in Action Define force and identify its different types (e.g., gravity, friction, magnetic). What makes things move or stop? Critical Thinking, Inquiry-Based Learning; Curiosity, Accuracy; Financial Literacy. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, magnets, smooth/rough surfaces, springs, weights. Oral questions, Written exercise. Relate to everyday experiences.
9 18 Force and Energy Pressure Explain the concept of pressure and factors affecting it. Why does a sharp knife cut better than a blunt one? Problem Solving, Critical Thinking; Logic, Resourcefulness; Entrepreneurship Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, pins, blocks of wood, sand tray, syringes. Practical demonstration, Written exercise. Pressure = Force/Area.
10 19 Matter Acids, Bases and Indicators Identify common acids and bases found at home and in school. What makes some substances sour or bitter? Critical Thinking, Communication; Safety, Responsibility; Safety Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, lemon juice, vinegar, soap solution, baking soda, safety goggles. Oral questions, Observation. Emphasize safe handling.
10 20 Matter Acids, Bases and Indicators Demonstrate the use of indicators to distinguish between acids and bases. How can we test if a substance is an acid or a base? Scientific Investigation, Problem Solving; Accuracy, Collaboration; Safety Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, litmus paper, universal indicator, red cabbage juice (homemade indicator), various solutions. Practical (testing substances), Written report. Introduce pH scale simply.
11 21 Living Things Health and Hygiene Explain the importance of personal hygiene and sanitation in preventing diseases. Why is cleanliness important for our health? Communication, Self-Management; Responsibility, Health Consciousness; Health Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, hygiene charts, posters, real-life examples. Oral questions, Group discussion. Connect to common infections.
11 22 Force and Energy Simple Machines Identify different types of simple machines and their uses. How do simple machines make work easier? Critical Thinking, Problem Solving; Ingenuity, Resourcefulness; Entrepreneurship Education. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, models of levers, pulleys, inclined planes, screws, wedges, wheels and axles. Observation, Written exercise. Relate to tools used in daily life.
12 23 All Strands Revision Review key concepts from all strands covered in the term. What are the main concepts we have learned this term? Critical Thinking, Self-Management; Diligence, Accountability; Study Skills. KICD Integrated Science Learner's Book Grade 8, past papers, revision notes, class activities. Oral questions, Group revision activities. Prepare learners for end-term assessment.
12 24 END-TERM ASSESSMENT Comprehensive assessment of Term 1 learning outcomes.
